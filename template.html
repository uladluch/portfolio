Headline: One Sentence Naming the Product, Your Move, and the Number It Moved.

Block: Text

Heading plus one paragraph. The plainest block — connective tissue between visual moments, or a claim that doesn't need proof: framing the stakes, stating a decision's thesis, closing a thought. From markdown this is a plain ## Heading followed by a paragraph. One idea per block — a second paragraph usually wants its own heading instead.

A figure is optional on every block, this one included — add one below the paragraph wherever a single image carries the point on its own: the product family, a results dashboard, a before/after. Leave it out and the block is just text.

Block: List

Heading, paragraph, bullet list — for anything that reads better as a claim than a picture: outcome metrics, a short set of constraints, a closing summary. Every number in it carries its period and its baseline; a number without a baseline is decoration.

  • First item: the headline fact, with the period it covers and what it was before.
  • Second item: a supporting fact — with honest attribution where more than one party moved it.
  • Third item: the ceiling. Past three, items start diluting each other.

A figure is optional here too — a chart or dashboard behind the numbers, dropped in wherever it reads better than a bare list.

Block: Image Only

Just a figure — no heading, no text. This is the one block where the image isn't optional; it's for the moment a picture has to stand completely alone between two blocks of prose, with nothing explaining it.
