Connect Gemini CLI

Bridge your mobile app with Gemini CLI on your computer.

Link valid for 15:00
1. Open Terminal
Install Gemini CLI (if needed)
npm i -g @google/gemini-cli
Sign in to your Google account (if needed)
gemini
Finish login successfully in Terminal before continuing.
2. Go to your project folder
Gemini runs in this directory. Example:
cd /path/to/your-project
3. Start the bridge — it connects automatically
npx antigravity-gemini-bridge@latest
